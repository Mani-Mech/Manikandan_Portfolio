HelioGrid


HelioGrid India Pvt Ltd, based in Coimbatore, specializes in rooftop solar installations and solar pumps for agriculture. With over 13 years of experience, it provides end-to-end services including design, certified installation, financing, and integration with schemes like PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, helping households and farmers reduce electricity costs and gain energy independence. In the USA, Matt Sparks’ HelioGrid focuses on solar financing for low-to-moderate-income communities, emphasizing solar+storage solutions. Historically, in Hungary, HelioGrid announced a 50 MW thin-film solar module manufacturing plant in Nógrád county, aimed at boosting renewable energy and creating jobs.
